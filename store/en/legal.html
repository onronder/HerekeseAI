The product and how it is delivered

AI for Everyone is a digital, interactive book read online through your account at book.onuronder.com. There is no physical delivery and no downloadable file: once your payment is confirmed, access is granted to the account whose email address you used, usually within a few hours and always within 24. You will receive a confirmation email when the book is unlocked.

Payments

Payments are collected through iyzico, a licensed Turkish payment institution, on iyzico’s own secure pages. Your card details never reach this site. Charges are made in Turkish lira; your bank determines any currency conversion and its rate.

Withdrawal and refunds

Under Turkish distance-sales regulations, the right of withdrawal does not apply to digital content once performance has begun with the consumer’s approval — that is, once the book has been unlocked on your account. For this reason we make the first three topics of the book, including their interactive demos, freely available before purchase. If you believe a payment was made in error, or the book was not unlocked within 24 hours, contact us and we will make it right.
