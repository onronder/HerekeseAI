Withdrawal and refunds

Under Turkish distance-sales regulations, the right of withdrawal does not apply to digital content once performance has begun with the consumer’s approval — that is, once the book has been unlocked on your account. For this reason we make the first three topics of the book, including their interactive demos, freely available before purchase. If you believe a payment was made in error, or the book was not unlocked within 24 hours, contact us and we will put it right.
