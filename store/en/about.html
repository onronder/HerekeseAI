About

The book

AI for Everyone is an interactive digital book that tells the two-century story of artificial intelligence — from the first calculating gears to today’s chatbots — in language anyone can follow. Across eight chapters, 45 live demos let you try every idea with your own hands, and each topic can be read at two depths, Simple and Technical. The book is read online at book.onuronder.com, with the English and Turkish editions included together; the first three topics, demos and all, are free to try before you buy.
